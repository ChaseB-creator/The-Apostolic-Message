ORIGIN

    Apostle Matthias Verses:

  • Acts 1:21-22: "Wherefore of these men which have companied with us all the time that the Lord Jesus went in and out among us, beginning from the baptism of John, unto that same day that he was taken up from us, must one be ordained to be a witness with us of his resurrection."

  • Acts 1:24-26: "Thou, Lord, which knowest the hearts of all men, shew whether of these two thou hast chosen... And the lot fell upon Matthias; and he was numbered with the eleven apostles."

  • Traditions of Matthias (Stromata II.9): "Wonder at that which is before you! For in holy wonder the soul awakens to behold the hidden majesty of God."

  • Traditions of Matthias (Stromata III.4): "We must strive against the flesh, yielding no license to its lusts, that our spirit may flourish through faith and heavenly knowledge."

  • Traditions of Matthias (Stromata VII.13): "If the neighbor of a believer stumbles into sin, the believer shares the burden; for had he walked worthily of Christ, his example would have turned his neighbor from iniquity."

  • Apostolic Preaching of Matthias: "The Son of God called us not to rule over men in pride, but to be servants of the meek, comforters to the afflicted, and bearers of the light of truth."

  • Martyrdom of St. Matthias: "I praise Thee, O Lord Christ, who hast counted me worthy to bear Thy cross and to be numbered among Thy holy Twelve. Receive my spirit into Thy eternal peace."
