ORIGIN

    Apostle James (Son of Alphaeus) Verses:

  • Acts 15:19-20: "Wherefore my sentence is, that we trouble not them, which from among the Gentiles are turned to God: but that we write unto them, that they abstain from pollutions of idols, and from fornication, and from things strangled, and from blood."

  • Acts 15:13-14: "And after they had held their peace, James answered, saying, Men and brethren, hearken unto me: Simeon hath declared how God at the first did visit the Gentiles, to take out of them a people for his name."

  • Acts 21:20: "And when they heard it, they glorified the Lord, and said unto him, Thou seest, brother, how many thousands of Jews there are which believe; and they are all zealous of the law."

  • James 3:17: "But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy."

  • James 5:7-8: "Be patient therefore, brethren, unto the coming of the Lord. Behold, the husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth... Be ye also patient; stablish your hearts: for the coming of the Lord draweth nigh."

  • Hegesippus / Eusebius II.23: "Why do ye ask me concerning Jesus the Son of Man? He Himself sitteth in heaven at the right hand of the Great Power, and shall come on the clouds of heaven!"

  • Eusebius II.23: "I beseech Thee, Lord God and Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do."
