ORIGIN

    Apostle Andrew Verses:

  • John 1:41: "We have found the Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ."

  • John 6:8-9: "There is a lad here, which hath five barley loaves, and two small fishes: but what are they among so many?"

  • John 12:20-22: "Philip cometh and telleth Andrew: and again Andrew and Philip tell Jesus: Sir, we would see Jesus."

  • Passion of St. Andrew 10: "O cross most welcome and long-desired, take me from among men and restore me to my Master, that by thee He may receive me who by thee redeemed me."

  • Acts of Andrew 14: "If I feared the terror of the cross, I would not preach the majesty of the cross. For the mystery of the cross surpasses all earthly glory."

  • Martyrdom of Andrew 5: "Every day upon the altar of the living God, I offer not the flesh of bulls or blood of goats, but the spotless Lamb that was slain, whose body and blood feed the faithful."

  • Apostolic Traditions of Andrew: "Stand fast, my beloved brethren, in the faith once delivered to you; love one another, and let no worldly terror shake your confidence in Jesus Christ."
