ORIGIN

    Apostle Simon the Zealot Verses:

  • Apostolic Confession of Simon: "No mortal sword or worldly empire can redeem the soul of man. Jesus Christ alone is our King, and His Kingdom is not of this world."

  • John 2:17 / Apostolic Meditation: "The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up. In Christ, my fiery zeal is no longer for earthly vengeance, but for the purity of God’s living temple."

  • Passion of Simon and Jude: "We come not with weapons of brass or iron, but bearing the invincible sign of the cross to dispel demons and bring peace unto your souls."

  • Apostolic Preaching of Simon: "If a disciple burn with zeal yet lack the charity of Christ, his fire consumeth his brethren rather than blessing them. Love is the fulfillment of all holy zeal."

  • Romans 12:11 / Apostolic Admonition: "Not slothful in business; fervent in spirit; serving the Lord; rejoicing in hope; patient in tribulation; continuing instant in prayer."

  • Martyrdom of St. Simon: "You may cut down this mortal flesh, but you cannot separate my soul from the love of Jesus Christ my Lord."

  • Doxology of Simon the Zealot: "Unto Him who broke the bonds of sin upon the tree and established peace between heaven and earth, be glory and honor forever and ever. Amen."
